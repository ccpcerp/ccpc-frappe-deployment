### Same-Company limitation

For R0, only stock owned by the issuing legal Company and consumed by a beneficiary Department of that **same** Company is within go-live scope. This owner-approved restriction must be enforced **on the server during real issue**, with role/Company/Department and original source checks; hiding intercompany buttons is insufficient. An issuer-Company stock issue for another Company's beneficiary (e.g., CCPC stock consumed by Ricor) does not, without Finance-authorized intercompany treatment, prove the other Company's expense/payable. Preserve all prior historical DEV records.
