## 2. Approved original-cost valuation; posting and exception handling still require accounting review

**Approved valuation basis (owner, 2026-10-17):** an accepted return of unused identifiable material **must be valued against its original posted issue cost** to reduce the original Equipment/Department net material consumption (not an arbitrary current list/standard price). This is an agreed business rule, not evidence that ERPNext will automatically post precisely that incoming valuation. Compute source issue cost from actual submitted Stock Ledger valuation (and its stock-UOM quantity) with a deterministic rule for multiple ledger rows, batches, precision, later reposts and changes in valuation. Cap total return value/quantity per source row and reconcile to its remaining unreversed original consumption amount. If ERPNext stock valuation or subsequent reposting produces a different incoming inventory value, **stop or route the discrepancy for accounting review**; document the proposed **variance account, dimension and authorizer** for separate approval instead of silently distorting the original expense. Whether to force the original outgoing unit cost as the incoming receipt rate using supported Stock Entry fields is a **test question**, not assumed verified behavior.

**Recommended account/dimension allocation:** before submitting a return, resolve the exact original issue item's expense-account and cost-center posting, together with its immutable Equipment dimension and original **beneficiary** Department (work_for_department when applicable). Preserve the source issue detail ID and relevant item/stock-UOM and warehouse/company references. The return's expense-credit entry should use that source account and those source dimensions. Do not recompute from current Item defaults, renamed/reassigned Equipment, current Department or Company fallback. Company and approved stockroom destination inventory account must be consistent. Whether Department itself is a registered GL accounting dimension versus represented through an approved Cost Center mapping remains to be inspected/tested in the target configuration.

**Illustration, not test evidence:** 10 bearings issued at 500 per unit: stock inventory credit 5,000; approved consumption expense debit 5,000 to original Equipment and beneficiary cost allocation. Three accepted unused bearings returned at the approved original unit cost: inventory debit 1,500; the **same original expense** credit 1,500 with same allocation; total net expense 3,500 and 7 net units consumed. A later-period return credits expense in its posting period; do not backdate or silently rewrite the original issue.

**Critical prerequisite:** verify/fix the original **issue-side** expense_account, cost_center and Equipment/beneficiary Department mapping in the dedicated branch and tests before asserting that the return can correctly reverse it. An issue posting to a generic default and a return posting to a different account would invalidate per-Equipment/Department reporting even when overall GL balances.

### Approved valuation decision — 2026-10-17

The owner explicitly approved original issued-cost valuation after the Option A discussion. For each submitted, legitimate and eligible partial return, the policy target is **accepted returned stock-UOM quantity × original posted issue unit cost** from the exact original submitted Material Issue detail. Restore accepted inventory, and reduce the **same original Equipment and beneficiary Department** net material consumption by that value. The 10 × ₱500 / return 3 example therefore targets a ₱1,500 expense reduction (net ₱3,500), contingent on real SLE and GL entries.

**Accounting exception checkpoint:** Any rate, inventory valuation or account discrepancy requires accounting review and documented approval **before posting**; there is no blanket authorization for an arbitrary manual rate, a new variance account, duplicate Journal Entries or overriding core ERPNext posting. Special cases including multiple source SLEs, valuation reposts, cost corrections, UOM conversions and stock-valuation methods must be resolved and tested. **The accounting entry mechanics, roles, and custom return implementation remain unapproved.**

## 3. Approved independent receipt roles; proposed remaining stockroom controls

- **APPROVED (2026-10-17):** Returning staff presents original Withdrawal Slip/item and quantity for physical handover. An authorized **Stockroom Personnel** user receives/verifies materials and records identity, timestamp, accepted quantity and condition. A **different authorized stockroom supervisor** independently reviews/approves and **submits the Material Receipt**. Deny server-side self-approval, direct bypass of receipt verification, and any draft stock/GL effect before approval. Preserve both users' identities/timestamps. Exact Frappe Role mapping, supervisor assignment, separation from preparer/issuer if required, cancellation authority and permission matrices remain unverified/not approved as implementation; do not infer existing role names or assign new roles.
- **APPROVED return-condition rule (2026-10-17):** Normal Material Receipt to usable stock is restricted to **unused, identifiable, serviceable, correct-company** material after inspection and independent supervisor approval. Damaged, partially used, contaminated, questionable, scrap, or otherwise suspect material must follow **separate inspection and suitable segregation/quarantine/rejection** and must not automatically credit usable inventory or reverse the original expense. Record physical custody without pretending that custody transfer is a submitted available-stock receipt. Specific serial/batch checks, salvage, disposal, quarantine warehouse assignment, hazardous safety and financial disposition remain separately unapproved.
- Source-link each receipt to the exact original submitted **Material Issue detail + Withdrawal Slip item** with immutable source company, Item and stock UOM. A distinct return-source header and child reference must coexist with the old **issue-only withdrawal_slip** rule; do not relax the issue validator into allowing arbitrary submitted receipts against it.
- Allow one or multiple partial returns only up to **issued units minus accepted, non-cancelled returns** (by exact source row). No return against cancelled issues, wrong company, stale warehouse eligibility, invalid UOM or changed source context. Use a unique return request identifier, SQL uniqueness where appropriate, source row lock, atomic submission and post-commit cache refresh for retry safety.
- Derive returned_qty and net_used_qty from submitted linked return vouchers, never user-editable totals. Cancelling a **return** reverses only that return's stock/GL and recomputes its contribution; original issue remains intact. Proposed safe default: **block original issue cancellation while any active return exists**, then use a separately approved reversal order. Do not auto-cascade cancellation.
- Account and valuation overrides must not be open to the ordinary return receiver or be silently altered by Item defaults. A privileged correction/exception path should log reason, creator, approver and source values.

### Owner approval: stock condition and quarantine gate — 2026-10-17

The owner approved **only unused, identifiable, serviceable materials** returning directly to **available inventory**, after Stockroom Personnel inspection and independent authorized supervisor approval. **Damaged, partially used, contaminated, questionable, or unusable** materials must be inspected separately and appropriately quarantined/segregated or rejected; there is **no automatic Material Receipt into usable stock and no automatic Equipment/Department expense credit** for these exceptions. Logging physical custody alone is not a stock-ledger or expense reversal transaction.

Design tests must cover attempted false serviceable flags, receiver/approver disagreement, forged condition fields, wrong-company/warehouse, suspect serial/batch provenance, partial condition-based acceptance of a mixed return, and failures that prevent unsafe inventory/GL effects. Quarantine stock accounting, salvage and disposal remain future separate owner/accounting decisions (and any hazardous handling requires suitable safety review); do not silently route such items into available stock.

### Explicit owner approval of receiver/approver separation — 2026-10-17

The owner approved the proposed **two-person physical return control**, not merely a recommendation: (1) designated Stockroom Personnel physically receives and verifies returned items; (2) a **different** authorized stockroom supervisor independently reviews, approves, and submits the linked Material Receipt. The receiving user's Frappe identity must differ from the approving/submitting user's identity; enforce this on the server and in direct API/Stock Entry posting pathways, not just UI. Preserve the real human receiver/approver, verification/approval timestamps, source Slip, condition and accepted stock quantity as audit data. No unapproved stock/GL recognition should occur at the first verification step.

**Unverified/undecided:** existing role labels and permission mappings, specific supervisor designation, handling supervisor absence, special-case corrections and cancellation of approved returns. These are implementation/configuration and accounting controls needing separate review. No roles created, no posting code implemented and no physical environment modified.

### Approved multiple partial returns — 2026-10-17

Owner approved separate, repeatable physical returns of eligible unused materials, each verified by Stockroom Personnel and independently approved by a different authorized supervisor. The total quantity of submitted, valid returns against each original issued Stock Entry detail must remain at or below that issue quantity. For 10 units issued, two accepted returns of 2 and 3 leave 5 net consumed; a further return of 6 is prohibited. Original issued-cost valuation and original Equipment/beneficiary Department allocation still require genuine accounting verification.

Implementation must retain separately numbered source-linked receipts, count only valid submitted returns, and prevent duplicate or concurrent approvals from exceeding the outstanding balance. The owner has since approved supervisor-controlled return cancellation and a source-issue cancellation block while active linked returns exist; the exact role mapping and accounting exception handling remain unimplemented and untested.

### Owner-approved Material Receipt cancellation and source-issue block — 2026-10-17

The owner approved an **authorized stockroom supervisor cancelling an incorrectly submitted return Material Receipt**, subject to accounting controls and server-side permission checks. Cancellation must reverse **only** that return's stock, valuation and GL entries and update only its accepted quantity, leaving the original submitted Material Issue and all other valid returned receipts intact. Recompute the source Withdrawal Slip's returned and net-used quantities from submitted, uncancelled return vouchers. Preserve cancellation reason, supervisor identity, timestamp and linked source/voucher provenance in the audit history.

The owner also approved **blocking cancellation of the original Material Issue whenever any valid, submitted linked return remains active**. Resolve outstanding returns through their own controlled process before source-issue cancellation; there is no automatic cascading reversal. Failure to reverse ledgers correctly, stock shortfalls after reissue/consumption, closed periods, accounting mismatch, unauthorized cancellation or duplicate/replayed cancellation must fail safely rather than rewrite historical voucher/GL data. Separate approval is needed for exceptional stock/period adjustments and the exact Frappe role assignments.

**Required new isolated tests (not yet run):** partial-return cancellation with other active returns, independent SQL/SLE/Bin/GL reversal by original Equipment and beneficiary Department Cost Center, incorrect receiver/approver/canceller permissions, replay/concurrency, original-issue cancellation blocked until all returns resolved, and inability to cancel when receipt stock has been consumed. Previous 79/79 CI validates original-issue cancellation only. No real CCPC DEV/PROD posting is authorized.

## 5. Remaining policy decisions

**Approved:** Option A — original issuance is consumption, accepted unused return uses a **separately linked Material Receipt** and reduces original Equipment/Department net cost **using original posted issue cost**. Valuation deviations require accounting review.
