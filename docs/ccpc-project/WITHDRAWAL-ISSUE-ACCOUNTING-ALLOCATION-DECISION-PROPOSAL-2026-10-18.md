## Purpose and tested evidence

The owner previously approved physical returns under Option A: the original Material Issue must charge the **original Equipment and actual beneficiary Department**, and a later independently approved linked Material Receipt must reduce the **same original expense account, dimensions and valuation basis**. An incorrect original issue allocation would also contaminate the reversal target; therefore issue allocation is a prerequisite.

GitHub source: [CCPC issue builder `_build_material_issue`](https://github.com/ccpcerp/ccpcerp/blob/review/withdrawal-issue-accounting-ci-2026-10-17/ccpcerp/ccpc_erp/withdrawal_slip_stock.py) sets item, warehouse, amount, provenance and Equipment where fields exist; it **does not set** `department`, `expense_account` or `cost_center` on an issue row. A filled work-for Department is **not overwritten** by the equipment default. There is no verified master-data crosswalk between Department and Cost Center in the current inspected CCPC builder/setup.

## Decision A — beneficiary Department precedence (OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-18; GITHUB DRAFT IMPLEMENTED / 13 REAL CI PASS, NOT DEPLOYED)

1. The **actual beneficiary** is `work_for_department` when explicitly recorded and validated for the correct company. This is **not** the Slip's `department` unless it is in fact the beneficiary.
2. When `work_for_department` is missing but a valid Equipment Registry record exists, use `Equipment Registry.department` as the initial beneficiary. The existing Slip logic often fills this automatically; account for old records and missing fields separately.
3. When both explicit `work_for_department` and Equipment Registry Department are present but differ, **stop automatic posting for a documented authorized review**. Do not silently override either with the requesting Department or Equipment field.
4. When neither beneficiary source exists, **do not silently charge the requester**. Require a positively identified, validated beneficiary Department, or accounting-approved exception. A prefilled requestor Department is not a substitute for confirmation.
5. Validate company ownership, active leaf Department, and immutable approved source intent; snapshot the resolved Department onto the submitted issue detail/header where the active Accounting Dimension is supported. If the required dimension is not configured, stop for configuration review rather than claim correct GL attribution.

**OWNER APPROVED (2026-10-18):** The entire Decision A beneficiary-selection policy above, including precedence, Equipment fallback, mandatory conflict review and blocking when neither source establishes the beneficiary. Only business policy was approved; no financial posting implementation, configured roles, authorization-exception process or ERPNext site change has been approved. Conflicts require a separately authorized, audited resolution procedure before posting. Proceed to Decisions B–D for accounting review.

## Decision B — original expense account (OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-18; EXACT MAPPING AND IMPLEMENTATION PENDING)

Do **not** invent a CCPC consumption account or silently adopt the generic `Stock Adjustment` account solely because native ERPNext returns one. Accounting must supply/confirm the permissible **expense-account mapping by company and item/material category** (possibly approved Item/Item Group defaults), including active account type, expected P&L treatment, handling different businesses and exceptions. Preserve the **actual posted original expense account** on the source voucher for return GL reversal; do not recalculate later using changed default accounts. Missing/ambiguous or non-expense account mapping should stop the new controlled issue workflow, subject to accounting-approved treatment.

**OWNER APPROVED (2026-10-18):** For every controlled original Material Issue, resolve to an **approved, valid expense account for its Company and material/item category**. Do not silently accept an unrelated ERPNext generic **Stock Adjustment Account** fallback; allow that specific account only when it is explicitly approved for the corresponding material/category and company. If the account is missing, ambiguous, inactive, unsuitable for the intended expense posting, or cannot be verified against the approved mapping, **block posting and route for accounting review** instead of substituting a guessed/default account. Retain the original actually posted expense account for any later linked Material Receipt/reversal. This approves the business validation policy, **not** any specific CCPC chart-of-accounts account, Item/Item Group precedence, mapping table/schema, exception authority, historical correction, application posting change or live permission/configuration change. **Still undecided:** exact approved account numbers/names and account-category mappings, whether any existing Item/Item Group defaults are authoritative, approved exception procedure and treatment of past issues. No historical financial adjustments authorized.

## Decision C — beneficiary Cost Center (OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-18; EXACT CROSSWALK AND IMPLEMENTATION PENDING)

Native ERPNext `Department` and `Cost Center` are separate doctypes; standard Department JSON does **not** define a built-in `cost_center` field, nor does this CCPC issue builder provide an explicit Department-to-Cost-Center mapping. Accounting must approve a **company-scoped beneficiary Department → active, non-group Cost Center** crosswalk (and Section mapping if needed). Do not guess from name similarity or silently charge the Company's global default. Validate that the resolved Cost Center belongs to the issue company and is suitable for posting. Multiple legitimate Cost Centers under a Department need a defined selector and audit trail, not an arbitrary first-match.

**OWNER APPROVED (2026-10-18):** Every controlled original Material Issue must select an approved **company-specific Cost Center mapped to the actual beneficiary Department** (as identified under Decision A). Require a valid, active, non-group Cost Center belonging to that Company and suitable for the intended expense. If multiple legitimate Cost Centers apply, require **explicit selection of the correct mapped Cost Center and validation** before posting, with auditable context; never arbitrarily select the first. If no valid or unambiguous approved mapping can be established, **block posting** rather than silently use a generic Company default or requesting Department allocation. This is policy approval, **not** approval of any individual CCPC Cost Center, Department→Cost Center crosswalk, mapping storage/DocType, Section policy, selector UI, exception authority, historical reallocation or financial code/ERPNext configuration change. **Still undecided:** Whether to store mappings on Department, a dedicated CCPC configuration DocType, or standard supported ERPNext defaults; actual company/Department/Centers and Section policy are unverified.

## Decision D — Equipment operational maintenance-cost tracking (OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-18; NOT IMPLEMENTED)

**OWNER APPROVED POLICY (2026-10-18):** Use Equipment as an **operational maintenance-cost tracking dimension**, with durable, auditable links from Equipment Registry and Job Orders to original submitted Withdrawal Slip / Stock Entry issue rows, eligible separately linked physical Material Receipts, and associated correctly posted expense records. **Do not require or automatically create an Equipment General Ledger Accounting Dimension at this stage.** Maintain the approved Company, beneficiary Department, Cost Center, and expense-account posting rules A–C; Equipment operational attribution is supplementary and must not create a duplicate GL charge.

**Maintenance-management objective:** Provide equipment-specific net maintenance costs and reliability trends to help assess **repair versus replacement**, not merely GL balances. Design the future per-equipment report to show parts consumed net of legitimate returns, actual attributed labor costs when an approved labor rate/source is available, outside maintenance/service charges when reliably linked, repair counts, breakdown history, downtime, operating hours/production where recorded, monthly/annual/lifetime cost, and cost per operating hour. Clearly mark uncollected metrics as unavailable rather than assuming zero; avoid monetizing downtime without reviewed loss assumptions. Future repair-or-replace comparisons should consider replacement acquisition/installation, remaining useful life, energy use, productivity, reliability, and financial evaluation separately.

**Financial reconciliation and audit controls:** Source amounts must trace to real submitted stock/GL transactions and other approved posted documents; cancelled documents and original-cost returned material must be correctly netted, with links to the original issued row and Equipment. Keep requester Department distinct from beneficiary Department. Prevent duplicate counting when a purchase invoice and a linked Job Order represent the same repair; support correction/cancellation audit trails, company isolation and time-bound equipment-to-asset assignments. The Equipment Registry's company/department identity and Job Order equipment/labor-hours links are existing source structures, but a complete maintenance-cost report, labor rates, downtime metrics, and integration correctness **have not been implemented or verified**.

**Deferred decision, not prohibited forever:** Finance may later request an Equipment GL Accounting Dimension if direct equipment-level GL reporting is essential. That would require separate accounting approval, field/configuration and migration impact review, real-ledger tests, and a distinct authorization. Equipment already appearing as a custom voucher field **does not prove an Equipment GL dimension is active**; the disposable isolated CI site had none.

**Scope limit:** This owner approval selects the operational-reporting architecture only. It does **not** authorize transactional code edits, report deployment, GL/accounting-dimension creation, master-data updates, labor rates, reporting assumptions, role grants, or either CCPC deployment environment.

## Decision E — return source snapshot, cancellation and legacy exceptions (OWNER PRINCIPLE APPROVED, IMPLEMENTATION PENDING)

Option A's accepted partial returns must refer to the exact original submitted issue item, use the **original posted unit valuation basis**, and credit **the original issue's actual expense account, Cost Center, beneficiary Department and relevant Equipment accounting attribution**—not current defaults. Original issue survives; unrelated returns are untouched. No expense/stock effect until a valid independently approved return receipt is submitted. A mistaken return cancellation reverses only its linked receipt with audit history. If original issue lacks an authorized account/dimension or stock value differs due to native valuation, **stop and route to accounting**; no arbitrary compensating GL or guessed rate. Historical issues are not automatically rewritten. These are tests/design gates, not proven live ERPNext return behavior.
