## Owner-approved decision — Option A (2026-10-16)

The owner selected **Option A: Material Issue at release + separate linked Material Receipt for accepted physical returns**, rather than employee-custody warehouses and Material Transfer as the default withdrawal workflow. The following are **business requirements**, not implemented or tested return functionality:

1. A submitted Withdrawal Slip Material Issue records the full released quantity as consumption/expense under the approved company accounts, Equipment accounting dimension, and appropriate beneficiary Department/Cost Center or related allocation. The *requesting* department must not silently replace the *beneficiary/work-for* department when they differ.
2. Stockroom acceptance of unused, returnable materials generates a separately numbered **Material Receipt** linked to the exact original Withdrawal Slip, Slip Item and submitted Material Issue detail. The original Material Issue remains submitted/auditable; do not cancel it merely because items were physically returned.
3. The approved return must increase warehouse inventory **and reduce the net expense/consumption attributed to the SAME original Equipment and beneficiary Department/Cost Center**. For a correctly mapped original issue of 10 units at 500 currency units each, return of 3 units at an approved 500 rate would leave 7 units / 3,500 net expense and restore 1,500 inventory value. This is illustrative, not a confirmed ERPNext valuation or GL result.
4. The desired entry under an approved perpetual-inventory accounting model is **debit inventory asset / credit the appropriate original consumption expense**, preserving the original accounting dimensions. The policy to use **the original posted issue unit cost for an accepted unused return** is owner-approved; its actual native ERPNext receipt/SLE/GL behavior, original expense-account mapping, and potential differences between equipment-cost reports and GL are **not yet verified or approved**. Do not assume a plain Material Receipt automatically reverses the original Equipment or Department expense, or introduce duplicate/custom GL without an accounting design and genuine tests.
5. Derive returned and net-used quantities from **accepted submitted return vouchers**, not manual edits of `returned_qty`; support bounded partial/multiple returns, explicit return-voucher cancellation, receiver/approval evidence and immutable source links. Return quantities and charges cannot exceed the outstanding issued quantity/value under the approved valuation policy.
6. **Approved return-condition rule (2026-10-17):** Only **unused, identifiable, serviceable** materials from the same company and approved source may enter usable stock through a normal accepted return. **Damaged, partially used, contaminated, doubtful-condition, scrap or other nonconforming materials** require separate physical inspection and, when appropriate, segregated quarantine/exception handling; they must **not automatically enter usable stock or reverse the original consumption expense**. Serial/batch, wrong-company and hazardous exceptions need their own verification and accounting/safety decisions.

**Still pending before implementing any return posting:** Accounting review for valuation exceptions and any inventory-vs-expense valuation differences; technical verification of the approved original-cost basis with ERPNext's receipt/SLE/GL accounting; agreement on exact original-expense credit mapping and beneficiary Department/Cost Center allocation; mapping the already-approved cancellation and receiving/supervisor duties to existing Frappe permissions; detailed quarantine/disposition and accounting/safety exceptions; and transactional tests of partial returns, cancellation and concurrency. No CCPC DEVELOPMENT/PRODUCTION execution is authorized by this design approval.

## Owner-approved return valuation — 2026-10-17

**Decision (owner-approved business policy):** For eligible unused materials physically accepted under Option A, value the return and reduce the original Equipment/beneficiary Department material consumption at the **original posted issued-unit cost**, calculated from the linked, submitted original Material Issue's actual accounted stock value and stock-UOM quantity. For example, 10 bearings issued at ₱500 each and 3 physically returned should yield a ₱1,500 reduction of the original Equipment/Department net expense and a net consumption of 7 units / ₱3,500, **only if** supported by actual accounting and stock entries. Preserve the original Material Issue; create a separately linked Material Receipt.

**Approved safeguard:** Do not silently substitute current Item list price, a generic Item/Company default, or an unrelated warehouse/current valuation when the result differs from approved original-cost treatment. Any exception, valuation discrepancy (including different incoming stock valuation, revaluation, reposting or precision), or required adjustment **must be reviewed and authorized by accounting**. Record the exception, actual inventory value, correct expense/dimension allocation, and variance treatment before posting. Block or route for review when a reliable original issued cost cannot be established. Do not assume native ERPNext receipt rate controls or GL reversals have already been proven.

**Still unapproved:** Exact software implementation, permitted approver roles, detailed GL account/dimension mapping and exception/variance account, handling of serial/batch/condition edge cases, and actual DEVELOPMENT/PRODUCTION execution. The approval is **policy only**, not a successful test of real material returns and not approval for posting code.

## Owner-approved return cancellation safeguards — 2026-10-17

**OWNER-APPROVED BUSINESS POLICY:** An authorized stockroom supervisor may cancel an incorrectly submitted linked Material Receipt, subject to appropriate accounting and permission controls. Cancelling that **return voucher only** must reverse its own inventory/Stock Ledger and General Ledger effects, remove only that voucher's contribution to accepted `returned_qty`, and recalculate `net_used_qty` and the original Equipment/beneficiary Department net consumption. The valid original Material Issue and other accepted return vouchers must remain intact, with full audit history. The original Material Issue **must not be cancelled while any submitted, non-cancelled linked return is active**; require controlled resolution of those returns first, without automatically cancelling them.

**Implementation safety requirements:** Enforce supervisor authorization server-side on voucher/API paths, require a reason and audit identity/time, validate the exact return/source links, period/ledger integrity, resulting stock availability and accounting reversal before accepting cancellation. Fail atomically if any stock/GL reversal cannot complete. Do not silently delete ledgers, double-reverse on replay, or allow unauthorized users to cancel either source or return. A cancellation that would produce invalid stock balances or violate period controls requires a separate reviewed exception, not a forced reversal.

**NOT YET IMPLEMENTED OR TESTED:** The exact mapping of supervisor roles, ERPNext v16 reversal mechanics for independently linked physical returns, accounting exception authority and safe handling of previously consumed/reissued returned stock. The existing 79/79 isolated regression gate covers cancellation of the **original issue**, not these new return cancellations. This policy approval does not authorize application code, role changes, DEV/PROD database transactions, PR merge or deployment.

## Owner-approved multiple partial returns — 2026-10-17

The owner approved multiple separate partial returns of unused, serviceable materials against the same original submitted Withdrawal Slip Material Issue. Every return must have its own Stockroom Personnel physical verification and different authorized supervisor approval, followed by its own linked Material Receipt. The sum of accepted posted return quantities must never exceed the original issued quantity for the exact source item.

Example: 10 bearings issued at ₱500 each; returning 2, then 3 in two separate accepted receipts leaves 5 consumed, with proposed net Equipment/Department cost of ₱2,500. Each accepted receipt is valued using the approved original issued cost, subject to actual ERPNext stock and GL verification. A further return of 6 must be rejected.

Implementation must calculate returned quantities from accepted submitted vouchers, not drafts or editable tracking fields; preserve source issue and item links; reject duplicates, retries, stale quantities and simultaneous returns that together exceed issued quantity. Original Material Issue stays intact. Return cancellation and reversal permissions remain undecided. Documentation approval does not authorize application posting changes or DEVELOPMENT/PRODUCTION operations.

## Owner-approved return-condition / quarantine rule — 2026-10-17

**Owner decision approved:** Direct restoration to **available stockroom inventory** through the usual Option A accepted return is allowed **only for unused, serviceable, identifiable material** from the correct company/source, after authorized Stockroom Personnel physical inspection and independent supervisor approval/submission. An approved and actually submitted return may use the previously approved original issued-cost accounting basis, subject to the original expense account/Equipment/beneficiary Department and actual GL/SLE reconciliation gates.

**Items NOT eligible for automatic return-to-available-stock:** damaged, partially used, contaminated, questionable condition, scrap, or otherwise unverified items. These require a **separate inspection and, where appropriate, quarantine/hold or rejection**; recording physical handover or custody in an inspection log **must not itself** create an available-stock Material Receipt, decrease consumption expense, or write stock/GL. Any later authorized salvage/reclassification/scrapping, stock receipt to restricted quarantine inventory, and related valuation/expense adjustment require independently approved procedures and accounting/safety treatment. Do not quietly handle hazardous/contaminated stock in ordinary usable storage. Also verify serial/batch identifiers and prohibit wrong-company stock before any posting.

**Implementation evidence and open decisions:** Preserve receiver identity, condition findings, accepted/rejected quantity and handover reason; ensure a supervisor cannot override inspection without explicit exception controls. Exact classification, quarantine warehouse or non-stock custody mechanism, disposal and salvage authority, exception permissions and GL implications are **NOT approved** by this general condition decision. The prior 79/79 CI suite proves original issue/cancellation only. No app/DEV/PRODUCTION posting code or data changes authorized.

## Owner-approved two-person physical receipt control — 2026-10-17

**Decision approved by owner:** An authorized **Stockroom Personnel** user physically receives and checks a staff member's returned materials (original Slip/item reference, accepted quantity, condition, company and warehouse), recording their own identity and verification time. A **different, authorized stockroom supervisor** then independently reviews those details, approves and submits the linked **Material Receipt**. A receiver must not approve/submit their own verified return. An unverified or unapproved return is **not posted into usable stock and does not credit expense**.

**Implementation security requirements:** The receiver and final approver are distinct authenticated Frappe users; check permissions server-side for *each* action, company, source Slip, destination warehouse and return status; record immutable, auditable receiver and approver identities/timestamps; do not treat a UI-only button restriction as authorization. The original Material Issue remains submitted. A direct Stock Entry/API route must not bypass independent verification or submit stock without approval. Failed approval, unauthorized calls, duplicate requests and any stock/GL failure must remain atomic/rolled back. Special return-cancellation authority and approval revocation rules remain to be decided and tested.

**Pending role mapping:** The business duties were approved; the exact configured ERPNext roles, authorized supervisor identities/assignments, permission matrices and any required custom DocType/fields have **not** been verified or approved. Do not create/assign roles or implement live Stock Entry actions as a consequence of recording this decision. Existing 79/79 isolated CI tested original issue/cancellation, **not** this two-person return workflow.

The same Frappe user must not perform both acceptance and approval/submission for the same return. 5. **Multiple bounded partial returns and return-cancellation safeguards — OWNER APPROVED (2026-10-17).** Each verified and separately supervisor-approved linked Material Receipt contributes only its active submitted quantity toward the original issued stock quantity. Block cancellation of the original Material Issue while any active linked return exists. ## 3. Proposed transaction integrity conditions (pending decisions)

- **One source of truth:** Derive accepted `returned_qty` from **submitted, valid, non-cancelled linked return vouchers**, not manual field edits; preserve immutable source Slip Item, company, original issue row, stock UOM, original warehouse, posting author, receipt evidence, and any serial/batch numbers.
- **Permission and provenance:** Fail closed before inserting return draft or posting. Verify returner/receiver role and company/warehouse access via actual DocPerms; avoid broad `ignore_permissions` for already user-editable drafts, reassignments, or cross-company transfers. Capture physical receiver, return time, reason, condition and approval as audit data.
- **Idempotency/concurrency:** A stable return-request ID or nonce must be unique at SQL level; lock the source Slip/issue and return request inside one database transaction; two simultaneous submissions must produce **at most one** accepted return. Reject quantities exceeding the unreturned posted issue balance, stale status, duplicate reference or mixed-document provenance.
- **Accounting:** Require a validated submitted stock voucher, real SLE, Bin stock and valuation, and company-account-balanced GL mapping before updating Slip return totals. A failed submit/GL posting must roll back all rows and Slip totals. Cache invalidation should happen after commit only.
- **Cancellation/exception:** Cancelling an accepted return should reverse its own stock/GL, reduce only its own contribution to `returned_qty`, and leave the original issue intact. No silent deletion/rewriting of original SLE/GL history.
- **Never touch physical CCPC DEV/PROD** before separate verified target provenance, backups, restoration readiness and per-environment approval.
