## Owner-approved decision — Option A (2026-10-16)

The owner selected **Option A: Material Issue at release + separate linked Material Receipt for accepted physical returns**, rather than employee-custody warehouses and Material Transfer as the default withdrawal workflow. The following are **business requirements**, not implemented or tested return functionality:

1. A submitted Withdrawal Slip Material Issue records the full released quantity as consumption/expense under the approved company accounts, Equipment accounting dimension, and appropriate beneficiary Department/Cost Center or related allocation. The *requesting* department must not silently replace the *beneficiary/work-for* department when they differ.
2. Stockroom acceptance of unused, returnable materials generates a separately numbered **Material Receipt** linked to the exact original Withdrawal Slip, Slip Item and submitted Material Issue detail. The original Material Issue remains submitted/auditable; do not cancel it merely because items were physically returned.
3. The approved return must increase warehouse inventory **and reduce the net expense/consumption attributed to the SAME original Equipment and beneficiary Department/Cost Center**. For a correctly mapped original issue of 10 units at 500 currency units each, return of 3 units at an approved 500 rate would leave 7 units / 3,500 net expense and restore 1,500 inventory value. This is illustrative, not a confirmed ERPNext valuation or GL result.
4. The desired entry under an approved perpetual-inventory accounting model is **debit inventory asset / credit the appropriate original consumption expense**, preserving the original accounting dimensions. The policy to use **the original posted issue unit cost for an accepted unused return** is owner-approved; its actual native ERPNext receipt/SLE/GL behavior, original expense-account mapping, and potential differences between equipment-cost reports and GL are **not yet verified or approved**. Do not assume a plain Material Receipt automatically reverses the original Equipment or Department expense, or introduce duplicate/custom GL without an accounting design and genuine tests.
5. Derive returned and net-used quantities from **accepted submitted return vouchers**, not manual edits of `returned_qty`; support bounded partial/multiple returns, explicit return-voucher cancellation, receiver/approval evidence and immutable source links. Return quantities and charges cannot exceed the outstanding issued quantity/value under the approved valuation policy.
6. Damaged, consumed, scrap, serialized/batch-controlled, cross-company and otherwise unsuitable stock require separately reviewed eligibility, quarantine and accounting rules before acceptance into usable stock.

**Still pending before implementing any return posting:** Accounting review for valuation exceptions and any inventory-vs-expense valuation differences; technical verification of the approved original-cost basis with ERPNext's receipt/SLE/GL accounting; agreement on exact original-expense credit mapping, beneficiary Department/Cost Center allocation, stockroom receiving/verification/approval roles, return-eligibility/condition rules, and partial/cancellation handling. No CCPC DEVELOPMENT/PRODUCTION execution is authorized by this design approval.

## Owner-approved return valuation — 2026-10-17

**Decision (owner-approved business policy):** For eligible unused materials physically accepted under Option A, value the return and reduce the original Equipment/beneficiary Department material consumption at the **original posted issued-unit cost**, calculated from the linked, submitted original Material Issue's actual accounted stock value and stock-UOM quantity. For example, 10 bearings issued at ₱500 each and 3 physically returned should yield a ₱1,500 reduction of the original Equipment/Department net expense and a net consumption of 7 units / ₱3,500, **only if** supported by actual accounting and stock entries. Preserve the original Material Issue; create a separately linked Material Receipt.

**Approved safeguard:** Do not silently substitute current Item list price, a generic Item/Company default, or an unrelated warehouse/current valuation when the result differs from approved original-cost treatment. Any exception, valuation discrepancy (including different incoming stock valuation, revaluation, reposting or precision), or required adjustment **must be reviewed and authorized by accounting**. Record the exception, actual inventory value, correct expense/dimension allocation, and variance treatment before posting. Block or route for review when a reliable original issued cost cannot be established. Do not assume native ERPNext receipt rate controls or GL reversals have already been proven.

**Still unapproved:** Exact software implementation, permitted approver roles, detailed GL account/dimension mapping and exception/variance account, handling of serial/batch/condition edge cases, and actual DEVELOPMENT/PRODUCTION execution. The approval is **policy only**, not a successful test of real material returns and not approval for posting code.

## 1. Source-verified present behavior — not a real physical return

- In `ccpcerp/ccpc_erp/doctype/withdrawal_slip/withdrawal_slip.py`, `_validate_issue_tracking` validates `0 <= returned_qty <= issued_qty` for each Withdrawal Slip Item; `_set_issue_tracking_totals` computes `net_used_qty = max(issued_qty - returned_qty, 0)`. Those are **document fields and derived quantities**, not necessarily inventory movements.
- `STOCKROOM_ITEM_FIELDS` includes `issue_qty`, `physical_discrepancy`, `discrepancy_remarks`, and `stockroom_remarks`, **but not `returned_qty`**. `_validate_stockroom_controls` therefore does not itself enforce a stockroom-only edit path for the `returned_qty` field. Actual field-level DocPerms/UI/roles need separate review before assigning exploitability or asserting any user has permission.
- `ccpcerp/ccpc_erp/withdrawal_slip_stock.py` recomputes `issued_qty` from *submitted linked Material Issue Stock Entries*, but `returned_qty` is only read while calculating `net_used_qty`. The file does **not** currently create a linked Material Receipt, transfer, returned-item stock ledger movement, or GL credit/debit when `returned_qty` changes.
- `ccpcerp/hooks.py` installs Stock Entry `on_cancel` sync; [CI 38900855857](https://github.com/ccpcerp/ccpcerp/actions/runs/38900855857) verifies real **cancellation of the original submitted issue** on a disposable test site restores Bin quantity and value, reverses GL by account, and resets Slip issue state. Cancelling the original issue is a **different business event from returning unused materials after issue**. Do not cancel original issue merely to register a partial/physical return.

## 2. Approved model and remaining decisions before implementing real return posting

1. **Return business model — DECIDED (2026-10-16).** Option A is approved: treat materials as consumed/expensed upon Material Issue and post accepted physical returns as separate, authorized, linked **Material Receipt** Stock Entries. Employee-custody Material Transfer is **not** the selected default model. Return submission, expense-credit mapping and valuation remain to be implemented and verified.
2. **Who is allowed to receive, verify, and approve return** (Stockroom Personnel, receiving witness, supervisor, or different approval chain); which roles may create vs submit vs cancel. Direct `returned_qty` editing should not masquerade as a posted return.
3. **What kinds of return qualify.** Unused/resalable material back to its same company and approved warehouse versus damaged, scrap, wrong company, unusable, partially used, serial/batch-controlled, or consumed supplies. Define reject/quarantine/revaluation workflows separately.
4. **Valuation and GL account policy — original issued-cost basis APPROVED (2026-10-17).** Accepted unused materials are to reduce the original Equipment/beneficiary Department net consumption using the original **posted outgoing unit cost**, traced to the original submitted issue row; other valuation rates require separate documented accounting review. Confirm exact debit-inventory / credit-original-expense mapping and corresponding dimensions; ERPNext's native Material Receipt does **not automatically prove it reverses the original consumption/expense account**. No code or live accounting entry is implemented.
5. **Partial and multiple returns.** Whether repeated partial returns are permitted; maximum returned units = successful original issued units less previously accepted linked return units; whether to support cancelling a mistaken return without cancelling original issue; which Slip statuses to allow.

## 3. Proposed transaction integrity conditions (pending decisions)

- **One source of truth:** Derive accepted `returned_qty` from **submitted, valid, non-cancelled linked return vouchers**, not manual field edits; preserve immutable source Slip Item, company, original issue row, stock UOM, original warehouse, posting author, receipt evidence, and any serial/batch numbers.
- **Permission and provenance:** Fail closed before inserting return draft or posting. Verify returner/receiver role and company/warehouse access via actual DocPerms; avoid broad `ignore_permissions` for already user-editable drafts, reassignments, or cross-company transfers. Capture physical receiver, return time, reason, condition and approval as audit data.
- **Idempotency/concurrency:** A stable return-request ID or nonce must be unique at SQL level; lock the source Slip/issue and return request inside one database transaction; two simultaneous submissions must produce **at most one** accepted return. Reject quantities exceeding the unreturned posted issue balance, stale status, duplicate reference or mixed-document provenance.
- **Accounting:** Require a validated submitted stock voucher, real SLE, Bin stock and valuation, and company-account-balanced GL mapping before updating Slip return totals. A failed submit/GL posting must roll back all rows and Slip totals. Cache invalidation should happen after commit only.
- **Cancellation/exception:** Cancelling an accepted return should reverse its own stock/GL, reduce only its own contribution to `returned_qty`, and leave the original issue intact. No silent deletion/rewriting of original SLE/GL history.
- **Never touch physical CCPC DEV/PROD** before separate verified target provenance, backups, restoration readiness and per-environment approval.
